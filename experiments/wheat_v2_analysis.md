# wheat_v2 analysis: class-source confounding

Where each class's images come from, and what that does to per-class recall on a source the model
never saw. The tables are generated by `python -m zarai.vision.analysis_v2` from
`data/splits/wheat_v2.csv` and the MLflow experiment `wheat_v2`. Don't edit between the markers.

## Interpretation

All figures are from the tables above: 10 configurations, 120 LOSO runs. They show how source and class are tangled together in this data. They don't show which visual features the models use.

### Three of the five classes effectively have one training source

- `black_rust` is 98.0% CGIAR (287 of 293). The other 6 images are Kaggle's.
- `yellow_rust` is 93.3% Kaggle (1,351 of 1,448 training images). Ethiopia supplies the other 97. NWRD's 409 tiles are test-only.
- `septoria` is 78.3% Kaggle (349 of 446). Ethiopia supplies the other 97.
- Only `healthy` (4 sources) and `brown_rust` (3 sources) are spread across sources.

So when a fold holds out the main source of one of these three classes, the model has to learn that class from one other source, and often from fewer than 100 images (section 2).

### A class learned from one source doesn't carry over to a new source

Mean recall across configurations, from section 3:

| held out | class | trained on | recall |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ethiopia | `yellow_rust` | 1,150 Kaggle images | 0.006 |
| Ethiopia | `septoria` | 298 Kaggle images | 0.073 |
| Kaggle | `yellow_rust` | 82 Ethiopian images | 0.005 |
| Kaggle | `septoria` | 83 Ethiopian images | 0.005 |
| Kaggle | `black_rust` | 244 CGIAR images | 0.128 |
| CGIAR | `black_rust` | 5 Kaggle images | 0.037 |

No configuration does well on any of these. The best configuration reaches 0.021 on Ethiopian `yellow_rust` and 0.144 on Ethiopian `septoria`.

The Ethiopia row shows that the number of images doesn't fix this: 1,150 Kaggle `yellow_rust` training images still give 0.006 recall on Ethiopian stripe rust.

Classes trained on two or more sources hold up much better:
- `healthy`, trained on 3 sources: 0.874–0.984 on Ethiopia, IARI and CGIAR, and 0.582 on Kaggle.
- `brown_rust`, trained on 2 sources: 0.418–0.823.

### Misclassified images go to `brown_rust`

In section 4, held-out images from single-source classes are mostly predicted as `brown_rust`:

| held out | true class | share predicted `brown_rust` |
|---|---|---|
| Ethiopia | `yellow_rust` | 0.981 |
| Ethiopia | `septoria` | 0.725 |
| Kaggle | `yellow_rust` | 0.906 |
| Kaggle | `black_rust` | 0.872 |
| Kaggle | `septoria` | 0.715 |
| CGIAR | `black_rust` | 0.609 |

`brown_rust` is the only disease class with images from three training sources. A likely explanation, not shown here, is that the models learn "a diseased leaf that doesn't look like the source of `yellow_rust`, `septoria` or `black_rust`" as `brown_rust`. In other words, they classify partly by what the source looks like.

It also happens the other way round. On IARI, 0.509 of held-out `brown_rust` is predicted `yellow_rust`, and on CGIAR the share is 0.281.

### What a bigger model changes

Not much. The 10 configurations' mean held-out macro-F1 runs from 0.401 to 0.472 (`wheat_v2_results.md`), and the top five are within 0.019 of each other. That includes DINOv2 ViT-L with LoRA at 0.453.

On single-source classes, the range between the best and worst configuration stays near zero (section 3). A model can't learn what the training data never shows it: a second source for the class.

### Caveats

- **Label errors are mixed in.** The 111 photos in Kaggle's `brown_rust` that Ethiopia labels stripe rust are quarantined. But single-copy label errors can't be detected, and they may make Kaggle's `brown_rust` and `yellow_rust` look alike. This analysis can't separate label noise from appearance differences between sources.
- **The CGIAR fold can't test `black_rust`.** It trains on 5 images.
- **These are image-level results.** NWRD tile recall (`wheat_v2_results.md`) is low in every configuration, with fold means from 0.0008 to 0.15.

### Implications

- For `yellow_rust`, `septoria` and `black_rust`, the next gain is more likely to come from a second labelled source per class, or from expert-corrected labels, than from a larger backbone.
- `brown_rust` predictions on a new source are unreliable as a disease call. Treat them as "diseased, type uncertain" until a model shows otherwise.
