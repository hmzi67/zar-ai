## Conclusion (written by hand from the tables above)

No variant passes 5 of 6 controls; the best (`rehearsal_l2sp`, `rehearsal_lesion`) pass 2. The
few-shot attempts stop here.

Rehearsal fixed the collateral damage. Brown rust stays at 0.89–0.92 recall instead of falling to
0.06, and pooled macro-F1 stays at 0.92–0.94. But it didn't make the model learn stripe rust:

- With the rust inpainted, recall barely changes (ratio 0.91–1.00), and Grad-CAM lift stays at or
  below chance (0.61–1.10, against 2.77 before adaptation) in every variant.
- Cropping the lesions helps most (field leaf called yellow_rust falls to 0.33, CAM lift about
  1.05), but still nowhere near the criteria.
- Transfer to Ethiopian stripe rust is now about 2–5%, against 93% without rehearsal. Once the
  model can no longer call everything rust-coloured "yellow_rust", nothing it learned from NWRD
  carries over to another source's stripe rust. Healthy Ethiopian leaves called a disease still
  rise (3% → 9–19%).

Taken together: 25–50 NWRD tiles teach the model to recognise NWRD photos, not stripe rust. Fixing
stripe rust in field photos needs more varied labelled field data (several sources, with healthy
and other diseases photographed in the same conditions), not few-shot adaptation on one dataset.
