# Gemma 4 as a zero-shot wheat disease classifier

Generated by `python -m zarai.vision.vlm_zero_shot report` from configs/vision/vlm_zero_shot.yaml. Model `cyankiwi/gemma-4-26B-A4B-it-qat-AWQ-INT4` at `76373001e106` (4-bit AWQ of Google's quantisation-aware-trained `google/gemma-4-26B-A4B-it`; vision tower in bf16), served with vLLM, greedy decoding, thinking off. All numbers below were measured in this run.

**Scoring.** Gemma's answer counts only if it is JSON with one of the six labels and a confidence in [0, 1]. `unsure` and invalid answers are scored as wrong (a sixth "no label" prediction), so macro-F1 and recall include them; their rates are given separately. Macro-F1 averages the classes present in the set, as for the vision models (experiments/wheat_v2_results.md). DINOv2-B is `dinov2_vit_b14` full fine-tune, strong augmentation, mean over its 3 seeds: the pooled run on the pooled test split and NWRD, and each LOSO fold's run on its held-out source. Its numbers are recomputed from the logits the runs logged.

**In-context variants** put one labelled reference photo per class (5 images) from the pooled train split before the query. These references are excluded from every set they fall in (each is in its own source's LOSO test set). Because the references come from the pooled train split, the LOSO rows for in-context variants are not out-of-source: a reference may come from the held-out source.

Seconds per image: *batched* is wall time of the vLLM calls (64 images each) divided by images, over the whole run; *batch 1* is the median of one request at a time on the first 32 pooled-test images. Not run: in-context at the 1120 budget. At about 7.3k tokens per request its KV cache doesn't fit beside the 16.6 GiB of 4-bit weights on this card. Offloading 3 GB of weights to CPU memory would make room, but in a 128-image check it slowed zero_shot_280 from 0.53 to 2.39 s/image.
